## 🔬 Research Spotlight

> ### **GASLIGHTBENCH: Quantifying LLM Susceptibility to Social Prompting**
> *Accepted at NeurIPS 2025 · First Author · <1% acceptance rate · Algoverse AI Research*
>
> A **24K+ prompt benchmark** measuring LLM robustness against social manipulation
> techniques — false authority, flattery, and assumptive framing. I evaluated frontier
> models including **GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, Llama-3, and Mistral**, and validated an
> LLM-as-a-Judge pipeline against human studies with strong agreement
> (**κ = 0.72, r = 0.89**).
